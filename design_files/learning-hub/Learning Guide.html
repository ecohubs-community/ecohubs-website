About this guide
Who wrote it?
EcoHubs members who live in or have visited the communities cited, with review from two governance facilitators. Every factual claim about a named community is checked against that community's own published material.
Is it free?
Yes, and it stays free. No account, no email wall, no course upsell. If it helps you, correcting an error is the contribution we would rather have.
How is progress stored?
In this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data clears your progress. There is no login to lose.
Can I use it in my own group?
Yes — the text is published under a permissive licence. Print it, translate it, argue with it in your forming group's meeting. Attribution and corrections welcome.
