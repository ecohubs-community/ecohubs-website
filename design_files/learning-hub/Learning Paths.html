Learning/Learning Paths

Learning paths

Four ordered routes through the same material, each written for a different question: should I join one, or should I start one? Follow a path end to end, or lift one step out of it.

Choosing one

Start with the question, not the syllabus.

Two of these paths are about a decision. Two are about a craft you will need whichever decision you make.

IF You are curious whether community life would suit you at allTake Find Your Community — six steps, six hours, ends with an honest yes or no.
IF You already know it suits you and you want landTake Become a Community Founder — the long one, and the one that saves the most money.
IF Your group already exists and meetings are the problemTake Governance Fundamentals, then Building Community Culture.
