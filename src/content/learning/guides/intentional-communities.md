Nine lessons, in the order the questions actually arrive.

The first three are about understanding: what these places are, which forms exist, and how they decide things — because governance is what most reliably predicts whether a community will still be there in ten years. The next three are the practical middle: money, land, and how membership works. The last three are the ones people skip and later wish they had not — conflict, endings, and an honest assessment of whether this life suits you at all.

You can read it straight through in a little over an hour, or take one lesson at a time. Nothing here assumes you have visited a community, and nothing assumes you intend to join one.
